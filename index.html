The Project

Not a group of office buildings, but a complete community designed to improve the quality of life of the people who use it.

Four districts, connected so that people can work, live, interact and enjoy their daily lives inside one integrated environment.

01

Concept

The project aims to create an integrated environment that combines work, living, culture, and entertainment in one place. Rather than being just a group of office buildings, the project is designed as a complete community that improves the quality of life and provides a better experience for its users.

The project is divided into four main areas: large and regional companies, startups and small businesses, residential areas, and cultural and exhibition spaces. These areas are connected to create a balanced environment where people can work, live, interact, and enjoy their daily lives within one integrated community.

02

Location

The project is located in the north of Riyadh, at the intersection of King Salman Road and King Khalid Road. This strategic location provides easy access and strong connectivity to the main areas of the city. The total project area is approximately 396,000 square meters.
